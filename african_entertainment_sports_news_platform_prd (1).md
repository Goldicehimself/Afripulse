# Product Requirements Document (PRD)

## Product Name
AfriPulse (Working Title)

---

# 🌍 PRODUCT VISION

To build Africa’s most addictive, real-time cultural platform where users don’t just consume news, sports, and entertainment—but actively react, predict, debate, and shape conversations.

---

# 🎯 CORE POSITIONING

AfriPulse is not a news platform.
It is a **real-time culture engine**.

> “Where Nigerians don’t just read the news… they argue, predict, and shape it.”

---

# 🎯 OBJECTIVES

- Become a **daily habit app** for Nigerians and Africans
- Maximize **user engagement (reactions, comments, predictions)**
- Deliver **fast, localized, culturally relevant content**
- Build a **community-driven ecosystem**
- Achieve strong monetization through traffic and engagement

---

# 👥 TARGET AUDIENCE

### Primary:
- Nigerians (18–40)
- Sports fans (football-focused)
- Entertainment & pop culture audience

### Secondary:
- African users
- Diaspora audience

---

# 🧠 ADDICTION STRATEGY (CORE DESIGN PRINCIPLE)

The platform is built around 4 psychological triggers:

- Curiosity → “What’s happening?”
- Opinion → “I need to react”
- Competition → “I want to win”
- FOMO → “I don’t want to miss out”

---

# 🔥 CORE FEATURES (MVP + DIFFERENTIATION)

## 1. 📰 SMART FEED (CORE HUB)

A unified feed combining:
- News
- Sports
- Entertainment

### Features:
- Infinite scroll
- Category filters
- Trending prioritization
- Mobile-first design

---

## 2. ⚡ REACTION SYSTEM (HOT TAKES)

Every post becomes interactive.

### Reactions:
- 🔥 Hot
- 🧢 Cap (fake/false)
- 🧠 Smart
- 😡 Angry

### Additional:
- Agree / Disagree voting

👉 Purpose: Turn passive readers into active participants

---

## 3. 🏆 PREDICTION ENGINE (HIGHLY ADDICTIVE)

### Use Cases:
- Match predictions (win/lose/draw)
- Score predictions
- Event outcomes

### Features:
- Points system
- Leaderboard
- Daily/weekly rankings

👉 Drives daily return behavior

---

## 4. 🔥 TREND WAR SYSTEM

A dynamic trending system where users influence what’s hot.

### Features:
- Trending topics list
- Upvote / downvote topics
- “Battle” topics (e.g., Artist vs Artist)

👉 Creates viral engagement loops

---

## 5. 🎬 SHORT-FORM CONTENT (30-SECOND NEWS)

### Features:
- Swipeable news cards
- Short summaries
- Video snippets

👉 Fast consumption = higher retention

---

## 6. 💬 ADVANCED COMMENT SYSTEM

Comments are a core feature.

### Features:
- Threaded replies
- Comment reactions
- Highlight top comments

👉 Encourages debates and discussions

---

## 7. 🧑‍🤝‍🧑 COMMUNITY GROUPS (TRIBES)

### Examples:
- Football Fans
- Tech Community
- Entertainment Lovers

### Features:
- Join groups
- Group-specific feeds

👉 Builds identity and loyalty

---

## 8. 🔔 SMART NOTIFICATIONS

### Types:
- Breaking news
- Match updates
- Prediction results
- “You missed this” alerts

👉 Drives daily re-engagement

---

## 9. 🔍 SEARCH & DISCOVERY

- Search posts, topics, teams, celebrities
- Hashtags
- Trending keywords

---

## 10. 👤 USER FEATURES

- Account creation/login
- Profile system
- Save/bookmark posts
- Activity tracking

---

## 11. 🛠️ ADMIN / CREATOR DASHBOARD

### Features:
- Create/edit posts
- Upload media
- Manage categories
- View analytics

# 📊 KPIs (SUCCESS METRICS)

# 💰 MONETIZATION STRATEGY

- Sponsored posts
- Affiliate partnerships (sports betting, music)
- Premium (ad-free experience)

---

# ⚠️ RISKS & MITIGATION

| Risk | Solution |
|------|---------|
| Fake news | Content moderation + verification |
| Low engagement | Focus on reactions & predictions |
| High data usage | Optimize media & lazy loading |
| Scaling issues | Use caching & CDN |

- Local language support (Yoruba, Hausa, Igbo)
- AI recommendations

---

# 🔁 CORE USER LOOP (CRITICAL)

1. User opens app
2. Sees trending content
3. Reacts or comments
4. Makes prediction
5. Checks leaderboard/results
6. Leaves
7. Gets notification → returns

👉 This loop drives addiction and retention

---

# 🏁 CONCLUSION

AfriPulse is designed to dominate attention in Africa by combining:
- Real-time updates
- Interactive engagement
- Cultural relevance

It transforms users from passive readers into active participants—making it more engaging, addictive, and scalable than traditional platforms.
